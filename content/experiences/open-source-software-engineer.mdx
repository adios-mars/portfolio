## Overview

Working independently on open-source applications spanning mobile, web, systems networking, and applied AI tools. Focuses on building practical software tailored to everyday problems with minimal infrastructure cost.

## Selected results

- **Taxi Ale**: Built a zero-cost real-time minibus tracking and seat availability mobile application for Addis Ababa using Flutter, OpenStreetMap tiles, OSRM routing, and 30+ offline-indexed commuter hubs.
- **Addis Market Tracker**: Shipped a FastAPI and SQLite web platform + Telegram Mini App comparing daily staple food prices across Merkato, Atkilt Tera, Shola, and Piassa.
- **Veda Voice Assistant**: Engineered a multi-tier AI voice assistant compressing 16kHz audio to 32kbps MP3 (reducing upload payload volume by over 85%) with Deepgram/Gemini STT and multi-model failover.
- **Katch**: Delivered a collaborative web reading tracker and book discovery product with real-time reading clubs.
