## Overview

Katch is a community-driven reading tracker and social book discovery platform. It brings readers together with dedicated reading groups, interactive discussion channels, personalized digital bookshelves, and rich catalog management.

Built with React 19, Tailwind CSS v4, Firebase Auth, and Supabase, Katch provides a fluid and responsive experience designed to make tracking and sharing reading habits engaging.

## The problem

Most existing reading trackers either feel utilitarian and dated, or lack engaging community and real-time interaction features. Readers often track books in one app, join reading clubs on separate messaging platforms, and miss out on unified discussions centered around specific books and chapters.

Katch was built to unite personal library management and group discussion into a cohesive, modern web application.

## Key features

- **Personal Bookshelf**: Track currently reading, want to read, and completed books with intuitive organization.
- **Reading Groups & Clubs**: Form and discover reading groups focused on specific genres or titles.
- **Real-Time Group Chat**: Live messaging with emoji reactions, allowing group members to discuss chapters and share impressions in real time.
- **Authentication & Security**: Streamlined authentication and profile management powered by Firebase.
- **Cloud Database**: Fast, reliable book and conversation persistence via Supabase.
